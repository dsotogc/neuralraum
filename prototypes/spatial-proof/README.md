# Floor study 02

Open `index.html` directly in desktop Firefox. The pinned PixiJS 8.12.0 browser build needs access to `cdn.jsdelivr.net` on first load. No server, installation, audio file or input is required.

| Time | What to watch |
| --- | --- |
| 0–4 s | Locate the DJ between the speaker stacks. Three amber-edged figures emerge in sequence from the recessed doorway on the left. The leader wears a diagonal strap. |
| 4–15 s | Follow the trio to the right, toward the booth. A lone figure walks left across their sightline, nearer the camera. Watch the order of overlap and identify each arrival again as they separate. There are nine original dancers plus the trio; the DJ is separate. |
| 15–19 s | The three settle at different depths below the booth. Watch their feet during the last steps and as the upper bodies begin to sway. The room and camera stay still. |

The final dance continues. Choose **Replay** or reload the page to repeat the same entrances and crossing. Judge smoothness, grounded feet and recognition on the reference desktop; no performance threshold is assumed.
